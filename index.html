Who we are

Dedicated footwear management for temples and events.

JIVAAN Footwear Management provides organised shoe and slipper stand services for temples, spiritual programmes, religious festivals and large gatherings. We help event organisers manage footwear counters with a planned process for receiving, arranging and returning footwear to guests.

Our arrangements can include footwear stands, token-based identification, bag handling, trained counter staff and event-specific operating plans based on guest numbers, venue layout and programme timings.
